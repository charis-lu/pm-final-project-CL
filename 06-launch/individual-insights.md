## Friction points

_The biggest challenge in developing a product concert was trying to turn a promising idea into something that is feasible, reliable, and measurable without overbuilding it. I kept thinking about trying to solve the technical issue but didn't realize how many other processes are necessary to validate a product concept. In this class scenario (StreamLine) specifically, I immediately thought just a feature change can help but turns out the feature is not the primary issue. The primary issue to is the problem and understanding the frustration before trying to propose a solution. _

_____

## Key learnings

_During this course, I learned that a lot goes into successfully launching a product and you have to think about the whole product lifecycle and not just when it ships. As someone who has never worked or done anything in product management, it was pretty amazing to go from just developing a strategic product/idea into launching it and coming up with a go to market plan. TO be a good PM, you have to be able to make good product decisions under uncertainty.  _

_____

## Aha! moment

_My main "aha" moment during this project process was realizing that a product managers job is not to have the best ideas but to build the strongest evidence based path from the customer's problem, to a product decision, and finally a measurable outcome._
