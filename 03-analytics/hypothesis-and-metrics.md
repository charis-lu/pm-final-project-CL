## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** She scrolls for roughly twenty minutes, watches nothing, and closes the app — the exact "engagement plateau" the hook names, since the session itself looks active while producing zero viewing. Her stated resolution was to go back to a DVD, meaning the 15,000-title library actively lost to physical media she already owned.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Content searched then played: fell from 41% → 34% over six months (−7 percentage points). This directly shows that users who search for content are increasingly failing to convert that intent into viewing.

Pain: 8/12 users browsed without playing + search-to-play fell 7 pts (41%→34%).
Persona manifestation: Priya browsed ~20 minutes and watched nothing.
Business opportunity: Wanderers are 41% of the base and show a 22-point churn difference when Spotlight-exposed
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Priya is a Power User whose goal is confident, single-sitting discovery; her confirmed friction is spending ~20 minutes browsing without finding something worth watching, a pattern consistent with the broader 8/12 no-play research finding and the 41%→34% decline in search-to-play.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Spotlight removes the friction of spending ~20 minutes browsing StreamLine’s 15,000+ titles without finding something worth watching.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** I expect users to spend less time browsing without playing and more often start content they feel confident watching, increasing meaningful engagement and repeat usage. If that behavior persists, it should support stronger retention and reduce churn, particularly among Wanderers, who represent 41% of the base and show a 22-point lower churn rate when Spotlight-exposed.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Search-to-play conversion rate (the percentage of content searches that result in a play start) because t directly measures whether Spotlight is closing the discovery gap of turning “I’m looking for something to watch” into “I found something and started watching.” The current baseline is 34%, down from 41% six months ago, so will need to increase the share of discovery attempts that convert into viewing, rather than prolonged browsing with no play.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** 30+ minute session rate (the percentage of sessions reaching at least 30 minutes) because Spotlight should improve discovery without reducing meaningful engagement among StreamLine’s existing viewers. The current baseline is 11%, down from 19% six months ago, so the 30+ minute session rate must not decline further as Spotlight adoption grows.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** Evaluate Spotlight over 8–12 weeks after reaching sufficient exposure, with a minimum threshold of a 5-point improvement in search-to-play conversion from the current 34% baseline, while the 30+ minute session rate does not decline below its 11% baseline.

Scale: ≥ 39% search-to-play with the guardrail maintained.
Pivot: positive movement, but <5-point improvement after 8–12 weeks.
Kill: no meaningful improvement after 12 weeks or the 30+ minute session rate deteriorates below 11%.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on 8 of 12 research sessions showing extended browsing without playback and the 41% → 34% decline in search-to-play, I believe that reducing discovery friction for Priya, the plateauing loyalist / Power User, will increase confident content starts and meaningful engagement, as measured by a ≥5 percentage-point increase in search-to-play conversion, from 34% to at least 39%. I will protect the 30+ minute session rate (11%) and make a go/no-go decision after 8–12 weeks of sufficient Spotlight exposure.
