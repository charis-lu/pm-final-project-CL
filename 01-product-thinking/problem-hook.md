# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
If StreamLine does nothing for 12 months, its current stated problems such as plateauing engagement and rising churn will remain unresolved. The company would continue operating in a market where consumers have more competing services and where retention is increasingly important.

## Moment of misery
The user is currently forced to spend long periods of time searching for something meaningful and not able to find what they are looking for which leads to misery.

## Problem hook
StreamLine’s 15,000+ title library is making discovery harder for the viewers we most need to retain, as engagement plateaus and churn rises; with curated competitors gaining ground, we need to determine now whether a more focused discovery experience can protect engagement and retention.

## Value proposition
For Loyal and discovery-focused StreamLine viewers, we will Spotlight will turn a 15,000+ title library into a smaller set of trusted and high-quality choices because the engagement is plateauing, churn is rising, and curated competitors are gaining ground.

The strongest part is starting the business risks and issues but the weakest part is not quantifying the financial impact, size of the affected audience, or rate of deterioration. I'll sharpen it by asking “How much revenue or retention is actually at risk?" and providing evidence of how many valuable viewers experience the discovery problem, and how much business value is threatened by it.
