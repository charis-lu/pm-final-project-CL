# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If StreamLine does nothing for 12 months, its current stated problems such as plateauing engagement and rising churn will remain unresolved. The company would continue operating in a market where consumers have more competing services and where retention is increasingly important.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to spend long periods of time searching for something meaningful and not able to find what they are looking for which leads to misery.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** StreamLine’s 15,000+ title library is making discovery harder for the viewers we most need to retain, as engagement plateaus and churn rises; with curated competitors gaining ground, we need to determine now whether a more focused discovery experience can protect engagement and retention.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For Loyal and discovery-focused StreamLine viewers, Spotlight will turn a 15,000+ title library into a smaller set of trusted and high-quality choices because the engagement is plateauing, churn is rising, and curated competitors are gaining ground.

- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Yes, rising churn and plateauing engagement are directly tied to the health of the subscription business.
